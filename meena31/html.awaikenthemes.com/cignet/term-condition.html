Legal & Governance

Terms & Conditions

Clear and transparent contractual principles governing our gold savings schemes, vault bailment, and franchise agreements.

1. Purity Assurance & BIS Hallmarking

All physical gold items sold, minted, or redeemed by Shalom Gold are strictly 100% BIS Hallmarked in accordance with Bureau of Indian Standards standards, carrying a unique 6-digit laser-engraved Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) number. Customers are entitled to verify their items on our showroom spectrometer Karatmeters at zero charge.

2. Gold Savings Scheme Guidelines (1 to 5 Years)

Enrollees agree to pay fixed monthly installments on or before the due date. On completion of the requisite tenure (11 months for 1 Year, 22 for 2 Years, up to 55 for 5 Years), Shalom Gold contributes the designated bonus installment amounts in full. Accumulated maturity value must be redeemed in physical 22K/24K gold bullion or jewelry. Cash refunds of accumulated gold weight are strictly prohibited in adherence with sovereign regulatory guidelines.

3. Gold Hold & Grow Bailment Contract

Under the Hold & Grow scheme, deposited physical gold is held under legal bailment in Tier-IV insured vaults. The depositor retains 100% legal ownership. Shalom Gold pays a 2% monthly benefit calculated on prevailing 24K valuation. At maturity (12 months), the exact physical gold weight is returned or converted to desired denominations.

4. 100% Buyback Policy

Shalom Gold guarantees 100% buyback of pure gold weight at prevailing spot market rates without hidden melting deductions for all gold purchased or redeemed from our boutiques.

5. Franchise Relationship Terms

Franchise partnerships require a minimum capital allocation of ₹50 Lakhs. 60% of capital is deployed directly as certified physical inventory delivered to the franchisee's vault. 40% is held as a legally documented refundable security deposit.
